A pixel-art clicker game about sorting the potatoes that come down a conveyor belt. The game text is in Japanese.

Tap the bad potatoes, such as sprouting ones, to toss them off the belt. Tap the sparkling rare potatoes to catch them. Every good potato that ships earns you money.

Spend your money on part-time workers, air blowers, sorting robots and more, and the factory slowly starts running on its own. Upgrades raise your output, and the factory keeps growing, all the way to a second line and a station in space.

Every rare potato you catch goes into the encyclopedia with its own little story. Catching a very rare potato starts a fever that boosts your output for a while.

Once you earn one trillion yen, you can close the factory and get seeds. Seeds raise the output of your next factory. You also earn a little while the app is closed.

You can play offline. There are no ads and no accounts, and the app collects no data.
